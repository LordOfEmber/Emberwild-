What's new on The Goats

ARK: Survival Evolved
🐾

High-Level Dino Spawns

Wild dinos on this map spawn far above vanilla levels. Expect tougher fights, stronger tames, and bosses that actually put up a fight.

🗺️

Fjordur

The full Fjordur map — every region, every biome, every underground cave — played the way it's meant to be, just with the levels turned way up.

🌊

Flipped Water Speeds

On land, a Sabertooth is fast. In our water, it's faster than a Baryonyx. We reworked swim speeds so land predators can chase you into the shallows — don't get comfortable near the shore.

🥩

Tougher Taming

Longer knockouts, more narcotics, pickier food requirements. High-level dinos don't go down easy — come prepared or come back later.

What's new on Dragon Island

ARK: Survival Ascended
🗺️

Gigantic Custom Map

A brand-new map built from scratch, several times the size of a standard ARK map, designed around exploration and set-piece fights instead of reused terrain.

🎬

New Animations

Players and animals both got new animation sets — new movement, new attacks, new idle behavior. Combat and taming both feel noticeably different here.

✨

Special Abilities

New dino species come with unique abilities you won't find in vanilla ARK — not reskins, actual new mechanics per creature. Rates stay standard, so it's skill and gear that decide fights.
